## Why

Redaction of the answer typed under Other in the Decide dialog is silent: the owner sees no sign that the recorded ruling differs from what they typed (T-163).

## What Changes

- When redaction changes the typed answer, the pane toasts that it was masked, without the typed text or the secret. An unchanged answer shows no toast.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `plugin-ui`: new requirement "Masked answer is announced", after "Decide walk with options".
