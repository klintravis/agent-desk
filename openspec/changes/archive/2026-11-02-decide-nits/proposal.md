## Why

A `Decide T-n` press can reject unhandled when a toast inside the walk throws, and the docs say the ruling is recorded verbatim or as given although redaction and `desk decide` can change it (T-71).

## What Changes

- Both Decide presses swallow a rejected walk like the pane's other fire-and-forget calls.
- Spec, README and CHANGELOG say the ruling is recorded after redaction, not verbatim.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `plugin-ui`: Decide walk with options.
